- Prepares the 0.17.0 erasure completion contract's read path (#1037, part 2 of 3). An erasure's committed key ledger is now published to ClickHouse's erased-subjects table by the cleanup job before any sweep, under a per-workspace generation that only moves forward and that the job records only after a `FINAL` read confirms it. Every reader of person-linked event data — event reads, analytics totals and breakdowns, named reports, campaign measures and summaries, alerts, segment event and engagement conditions, journeys, campaign identity and warehouse exports — reads new protected views that exclude a row when any of its subject keys, or a backfilled side entry for it, matches an erased subject of the same workspace; rows written after the erasure are excluded too, and both halves of a pending-outbox union are filtered. Ad spend, which names campaigns rather than people, is unchanged. A guard test fails if a source file reads a person table directly outside the maintenance paths, and the analytics result cache is keyed by the workspace's publication generation so a pre-erasure result is never served afterwards. Completion semantics, `profiles.erasureStatus` and physical purge are unchanged until part 3.
