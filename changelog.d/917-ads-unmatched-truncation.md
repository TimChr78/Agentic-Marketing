- **Breaking:** `ads.performance` now distinguishes a conversion that had a campaign touch from one that had none. `unattributed` keeps its name and means only conversions with no campaign touch in the window; a new `unmatched` object counts the conversions whose last touch names a campaign with no spend row, and `totals` remains the sum of the campaign rows. A spend read that stops at its provider page ceiling is no longer silent: `ads.importSpend` returns `truncated`, and `ads.performance` returns `spendTruncated` with a note naming the incomplete dates, so cost is known to be incomplete and CPA understated while ROAS is overstated. The incomplete dates are kept per connection in a server-owned `connections.spend_state` column (migration 0095), never in the connector’s editable settings, so a later complete refresh of a few days cannot hide an older gap and a rename or settings edit cannot drop the warning. The page ceilings were also raised (Meta 200 pages, Google Ads 50).
