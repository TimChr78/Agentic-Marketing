- **Breaking:** `analytics.journeyFunnel` applies the k=5 floor to its step rows and its headline totals. A step below k hides the whole subtree under its parent in the published graph (every descendant, through forks, merges and loops), so a participant moving between branches under that parent cannot change any visible number; a below-k entry step, or any below-k step without a published version, hides every step row. Headlines are not modelled — how they relate to the steps cannot be known in general — so whenever any step row is hidden, every headline total (`entered`, `goalReached`, `completed`, `exited`) is withheld as `null`; a positive headline below k is withheld on its own. Over-suppression is deliberate, and a note names the hidden subtree’s parent or says every step row was hidden. Drop-off and drop-off rate with a hidden operand stay `null`. API clients must handle four more nullable fields, and the journey report renders a withheld total as suppressed.
